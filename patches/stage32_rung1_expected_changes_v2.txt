THE OPERATOR BEING MEASURED

  The iteration actually performed is

      S = S_fixed + (chi_es/chi_tot) * Lambda_formal[S]

  with chi_es assembled at src/lumina_cmfgen.c:1641 and the diagonal of
  Lambda_formal stored by production in cs->lambda_star
  (src/lumina_cmfgen.c:2147, :2179).  Therefore the local spectral radius is

      rho_local[idx] = (chi_es[idx]/chi_tot[idx]) * lambda_star[idx]

  computed from production arrays only.  No rate coefficients, no Sobolev
  two-level reconstruction, and no choice of epsilon definition enters the
  primary measurement.

FROZEN PREDICTION 1 -- magnitude

  For 600--3000 Angstrom bins at shell 8, the line-emission-energy-weighted
  median of rho_local lies in [0.90, 0.98).

  Anchor: E8 measured chi_coherent/chi_total = 0.977713 for s8 UV
  (validation/emiss_e8/summary.json).  Since lambda_star < 1 by construction,
  rho_local < 0.9777 strictly.  The lower bound asserts that lambda_star is
  not small in these bins, i.e. that the UV bins are locally trapping.

FROZEN PREDICTION 2 -- the discriminating test

  The local geometric amplification implied by the measured operator is
  1/(1 - rho_local).  Prediction 1 places this in [10, 50].

  E8's reported recycle gain is S_total/S_fixed = 5247.49, which would require
  rho = 0.99981.  That value is unreachable given chi_es/chi_tot = 0.9777.

  This preregistration therefore predicts a CONTRADICTION of roughly two
  orders of magnitude between the direct operator measurement and E8's source
  decomposition.  The two quantities are computed from disjoint arrays
  (chi_es/lambda_star versus eta_fixed/eta_coherent), so this is an
  independent second path, not a replay.

  Dispositions, fixed in advance:
    (a) gain in [10,50] and E8 stands at 5247  ->  E8's 5247 is a source
        composition ratio, NOT an iteration amplification.  The "recycle gain"
        language in UV_CENSUS_CONSOLIDATION and the paper must be withdrawn.
        This is the outcome this preregistration expects.
    (b) gain also ~5000  ->  Prediction 1 is falsified; lambda_star or chi_es
        is not what this preregistration assumes, and the assumption must be
        re-derived before Stage 3.2 proceeds.
    (c) anything else  ->  discovery; register it, do not tune toward (a).

  No clamp, floor, cap, or fallback may be introduced to move an observation
  into any of these ranges.

SECONDARY ARTIFACT -- per-line view

  The per-line Sobolev view is retained as a diagnostic, NOT as a prediction
  target.  Each row carries, for the same (line, shell, bin):
    beta            = (1-exp(-tau))/tau
    eps0_raw        = C/(C+A)           [no escape factor]
    eps_prime       = C/(C+A*beta)      [production's net thermalization,
                                         src/lumina_plasma.c:8471]
    eps_applied     = eps_prime after production's eps_floor/eps_cap
                      (src/lumina_cmfgen.c:795-797)
  All four are recorded so the two readings can be compared post hoc on one
  payload, and so the number of rows pinned by eps_floor is countable.  No
  prediction is frozen on these columns.

HARD-GATE EVIDENCE

  The EPAY disposition column is written at the branch site, never
  reconstructed.  Row energy is the value production adds to eta_line, reused
  and not recomputed.  The manifest closes both row-count and energy censuses
  by disposition.  Rung-1 values are written to a read-only side-band artifact
  and are never routed through eta_line/S_fixed.

No source function, opacity, emissivity, rate, population, or transport state
may be mutated.  No Stage 2+ implementation is permitted.
